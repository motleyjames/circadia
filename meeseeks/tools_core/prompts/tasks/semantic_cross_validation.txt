# SEMANTIC CROSS-VALIDATION ANALYSIS

You are performing multi-modal semantic validation across different analysis outputs of the SAME source video. Each output was generated by a different analytical lens (meeting, saas, logic, training-human, presentation, general). Since they all describe the same reality, they MUST be internally consistent.

## Input Context

You will receive multiple outputs from the same video chunk:

{outputs}

## Your Mission

Cross-validate ALL outputs against each other to identify:
1. **Contradictions** - Facts that conflict across outputs
2. **Gaps** - Information in one output missing from others where expected
3. **Inconsistencies** - Same concept described differently
4. **Timestamp Misalignments** - Events placed at different times
5. **Entity Discrepancies** - Names, roles, or relationships that don't match

---

## VALIDATION DIMENSIONS

### 1. FACTUAL CONSISTENCY
Compare concrete facts across all outputs:
- Speaker names and roles
- Software/system names
- Feature names and descriptions
- Timestamps of events
- Numerical values (prices, percentages, counts)
- Quoted speech

**Check:** Does Output A say "X" while Output B says "Y" about the same thing?

### 2. PROCEDURAL CONSISTENCY
Compare workflows, processes, and sequences:
- Steps in procedures
- Order of operations
- Prerequisites and dependencies
- Outcomes and results

**Check:** Do the logic diagrams match the procedures in training-human? Does the SaaS workflow match the meeting discussion?

### 3. ENTITY CONSISTENCY
Compare all named entities:
- People (speakers, mentioned individuals)
- Systems (software, modules, features)
- Organizations (companies, teams)
- Concepts (terminology, definitions)

**Check:** Is "Virtual Account" described the same way in saas vs. training-human vs. logic?

### 4. TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY
Compare timeline references:
- When events occur
- Sequence of discussions
- Duration claims

**Check:** Does the meeting transcript place a discussion at [14:00] while logic says [19:00]?

### 5. STRUCTURAL CONSISTENCY
Compare hierarchies and relationships:
- Data model relationships (saas)
- Logic flow dependencies (logic)
- UI navigation paths (training-human)

**Check:** Does the inferred data model match the entity relationships in the logic diagrams?

### 6. CONTINUATION & SEQUENCE INTEGRITY
Check for cross-chunk coherence and complete sequences:
- Sentence fragments at start/end suggesting continuation
- Mid-list items (starting with "5." suggests 1-4 elsewhere)
- Pronouns without antecedents ("The system..." but system not introduced)
- Lists that start/end mid-sequence
- Numbering gaps (Section 3 jumps to Section 5)

**Check:** Does chunk_02 pick up where chunk_01 left off? Are all sequences complete?

### 7. CROSS-REFERENCE RESOLUTION
Track internal references between outputs:
- "As shown in the logic diagram..." - does the diagram exist?
- "See workflow above..." - is the workflow actually above?
- "The procedure described earlier..." - was it described?
- References to timestamps - do those timestamps exist in meeting transcript?

**Check:** Can every reference be resolved to its target?

### 8. DEFINITIONAL DEPENDENCIES
Track terms used but not defined:
- Capitalized terms suggesting formal definitions (e.g., "Virtual Account")
- Terms in quotes implying specific meaning
- Jargon used without explanation
- Acronyms without expansion

**Check:** Is every significant term defined somewhere across the outputs?

### 9. KNOWLEDGE GAP TYPING
Categorize missing information by type:
- **missing_trigger**: Obligation without clear triggering condition
- **missing_consequence**: Condition without clear outcome
- **unidentified_party**: Person/role mentioned but not identified
- **unspecified_value**: Amount, date, or quantity referenced but not given
- **incomplete_procedure**: Steps that trail off or skip

**Check:** What would a reader NEED to know that isn't present in ANY output?

## CRITICAL RULES

1. **Assume consistency is expected** - Same video = same reality
2. **Quote verbatim** - Always show exact text from each output
3. **Never dismiss minor issues** - Small inconsistencies may signal larger problems
4. **Prioritize by impact** - Critical = affects understanding of core functionality; Minor = cosmetic
5. **Hypotheses must be testable** - Each hypothesis needs a concrete verification method
6. **Cross-validate timestamps** - Time references are high-signal for catching drift
7. **Track entity aliases** - Same thing may have different names across modes
8. **Note mode-specific blind spots** - Some modes won't capture certain information (that's OK)
9. **Generate validation queries** - Produce specific questions that could be verified against the source video
10. **Track undefined terms** - Flag any jargon, acronyms, or capitalized terms without definitions
11. **Detect continuation signals** - Fragments, mid-lists, pronouns without antecedents suggest missing context
12. **Resolve all cross-references** - Every "see X" or "as described in Y" must point to something real
13. **Type all knowledge gaps** - Don't just say "information missing" - categorize what KIND is missing

### Logic Diagrams vs. Procedural Descriptions

### Timestamps Across Modes
- Meeting and general modes have detailed timestamps

- Cross-check these align for the same events

### Terminology Normalization
- "Virtual Account" vs "Default Account" - are these used consistently?
- Speaker names - spelled the same way?
- Feature toggles - same names across outputs?

### Sequence & Continuation Signals
- Does chunk_01 end mid-sentence or mid-procedure?
- Does chunk_02 start with context that assumes prior knowledge?
- Are numbered lists complete (1-5) or partial (3-5)?
- Are there pronouns ("The system", "It", "They") without clear antecedents?

### Cross-Reference Integrity
- When training-human says "as shown in the Entity Map" - does saas describe that map?
- When logic shows a flowchart decision - does meeting transcript discuss that decision?
- When meeting mentions a timestamp [14:20] - do other modes reference the same event at the same time?

### Definition Coverage
- Every capitalized term (e.g., "Feature Access", "LOA", "Direct Connection") should be defined somewhere
- Acronyms must be expanded at least once
- Jargon specific to the software should have explanations
