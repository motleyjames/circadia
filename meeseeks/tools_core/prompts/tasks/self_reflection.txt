# SELF-REFLECTION PROMPT

Before completing this loop, take a moment to reflect and leave hints for your future self (or another Meeseeks that continues this work).

## IMPORTANT: This Is Not a Summary

Self-reflection is NOT about wrapping up. It's about:
- **Capturing insights** that might be lost between loops
- **Leaving breadcrumbs** for continuation (loops can go indefinitely)
- **Being verbose** - future loops will thank you for context
- **Noting tangents** - things you noticed but didn't pursue

The next loop (could be loop 2 or loop 15) needs your full context. Don't compress your thinking prematurely.

## What to Think About

1. **Incomplete Work**: What did you start but not finish? Where exactly did you leave off?
2. **Patterns Noticed**: Any regularities, structures, or patterns you observed?
3. **Warnings**: Gotchas, pitfalls, or things that tripped you up?
4. **Future Hints**: What would help if you (or another Meeseeks) encounter this again?
5. **Cross-References**: Did you notice something in one place that relates to another?

## Be Verbose and Specific

Generic hints are useless. Be specific:

❌ BAD: "There might be more data somewhere"
✅ GOOD: "The asset list on page 5 appears cut off at item 47 - page 4 likely has items 1-46"

❌ BAD: "Check the config"
✅ GOOD: "The DATABASE_URL in .env.example uses postgres:// but SQLAlchemy 2.0 requires postgresql://"

## Remember

- **Be specific** - vague hints are useless
- **Include locations** - file paths, line numbers, page numbers
- **Explain context** - when does this hint apply?
- **Rate importance** - critical/useful/minor
- **Think about continuation** - if you stopped here, what would help you restart?

The next Meeseeks (or your future self) will thank you! 🔵
