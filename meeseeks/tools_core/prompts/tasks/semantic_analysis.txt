## Analysis Tasks

### 1. CONTINUATION DETECTION
Does this page continue from a previous page or continue to the next?

Signals:
- Sentence fragments at top/bottom
- "...continued" or "(cont.)" markers
- Mid-list items (starting with "5." suggests 1-4 elsewhere)
- Section numbers without headers (suggests header on previous page)
- Pronouns without antecedents ("The Trustee shall..." but Trustee not defined on this page)

### 2. SEQUENCE INTEGRITY
Are there numbered/lettered sequences? Are they complete on this page?

Check for:
- Lists that start mid-sequence (e.g., items 6-10 but not 1-5)
- Lists that end mid-sequence (e.g., items 1-3 but "(continued)" or context suggests more)
- Article/Section numbering gaps

### 3. CROSS-REFERENCE MAPPING
What does this page reference that exists elsewhere?

Look for:
- "See Section X", "as defined in Article Y"
- "pursuant to", "in accordance with"
- Page number references
- Exhibit/Schedule references
- External document references

### 4. DEFINITIONAL DEPENDENCIES
What terms are USED but not DEFINED on this page?

Flag terms that:
- Are capitalized (suggesting defined term)
- Are in quotes
- Are used with specific meaning but no definition visible

### 5. KNOWLEDGE GAPS
What would a professional need to know that ISN'T on this page?

Consider:
- Obligations without clear triggers
- Conditions without clear consequences
- Parties mentioned but not identified
- Dates/amounts referenced but not specified
